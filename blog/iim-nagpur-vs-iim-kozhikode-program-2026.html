IIM Nagpur vs IIM Kozhikode — What You're Choosing Between

IIM Nagpur and IIM Kozhikode both offer high-quality executive programmes for working professionals. IIM Nagpur's primary differentiator is the Blended MBA — a full UGC-DEB approved MBA degree that no other IIM offers for working professionals. IIM Kozhikode has 4 executive programmes, higher NIRF ranking (#3 vs #25), and EQUIS+AMBA accreditation. Different institutions with complementary offerings.

When to Choose IIM Nagpur

  • You specifically need an MBA degree — IIM Nagpur Blended MBA is the only option in this category for working professionals
  • You want IIM credentials + data science / fintech focus at the most affordable price point (~₹1.75L)
  • You are based in Maharashtra and value the IIM Nagpur regional network
  • You have 3+ years experience (NAMP requires 10)

When to Choose IIM Kozhikode

  • You have 10+ years experience — NAMP is specifically designed for this profile; IIM Nagpur has no equivalent
  • You want India's 3rd ranked IIM brand with EQUIS accreditation at ₹2-4L
  • You want AI/analytics credential with strong IIM brand — IIM-K Analytics & GenAI (~₹2.1L)
  • You are in South India where IIM Kozhikode alumni network is dense

Frequently Asked Questions

Is IIM Nagpur or IIM Kozhikode better?

Neither is universally better — they serve different purposes. IIM Kozhikode (#3 NIRF, EQUIS+AMBA) has stronger brand recognition and NAMP is the best programme for 10+ year professionals. IIM Nagpur (#25 NIRF, AACSB) has the unique Blended MBA degree — the only IIM MBA for working professionals. Choose based on which specific programme fits your career goal.

What is the difference between IIM Nagpur MBA and IIM Kozhikode NAMP?

IIM Nagpur Blended MBA: ₹12.10L, 2 years, UGC-DEB MBA degree, requires 3 years experience, gives a full MBA degree. IIM Kozhikode NAMP: ₹4L, 10 months, executive certificate, requires 10 years experience, gives certificate. MBA degree vs certificate is the primary difference. Both give their respective IIM alumni status.

Can I do both IIM Nagpur and IIM Kozhikode programmes?

Yes — many professionals sequence IIM programmes. A common path: IIM Kozhikode Analytics (~₹2.1L, 6-8 months) first for quick IIM-K credential + AI skills, then IIM Nagpur Blended MBA for the MBA degree later. Or IIM Kozhikode Supply Chain (~₹1.75L) for domain credential, then general management programme later.
