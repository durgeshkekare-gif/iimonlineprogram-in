Why Apply to Batch 2 — Don't Wait for Batch 3

  • Seats are limited — each batch has a defined cohort size; early applications improve selection odds
  • IIMNAT preparation requires 6-8 weeks — late applicants have compressed prep time
  • Offer acceptance requires ₹1L deposit — financial planning needs lead time
  • Career impact starts 2 years earlier — every batch you delay is 2 additional years before the degree
  • Batch 3 (2027-29) dates not yet announced — Batch 2 is the confirmed current window

How to Apply for Batch 2

  • Step 1: Visit official Jaro Education page for IIM Nagpur Blended MBA
  • Step 2: Fill online application form — personal, educational, work experience details
  • Step 3: Pay ₹2,500 + GST application fee
  • Step 4: Upload documents — degree certificate, marksheets, work experience letters, ID
  • Step 5: Prepare for IIMNAT (19 July 2026) — 6-8 weeks preparation recommended
  • Step 6: Appear for IIMNAT → GD → PI rounds
  • Step 7: Receive offer letter → accept within 7 days and pay ₹1L booking

Who Should Apply to Batch 2 Right Now

  • Working professionals with minimum 3 years experience as of application date
  • Graduates with minimum 50% marks
  • Professionals targeting Director, VP, C-suite, or general management roles
  • Those who cannot do a full-time MBA but want IIM institutional credentials
  • Applicants ready to invest ₹12.10L + GST over 2 years

Frequently Asked Questions

When does IIM Nagpur Blended MBA Batch 2 start?

Campus Module 1 for Batch 2 is scheduled for 28 November 2026. Online classes begin in November 2026 post-offer acceptance. The programme runs through approximately May-June 2028.

How many seats are available in IIM Nagpur Blended MBA Batch 2?

IIM Nagpur has not publicly announced the exact seat count for Batch 2. Based on Batch 1 cohort size (approximately 60-80 participants), Batch 2 is expected to be similar. Apply early — seats are limited and fill up as GD/PI processes complete.

What if I miss the Batch 2 deadline for IIM Nagpur MBA?

If you miss the Batch 2 (2026-28) deadline, the next opportunity is Batch 3 (approximately 2027-29 — dates not yet confirmed). Missing Batch 2 means waiting approximately 1 year for the next application window and starting 2 years later. Apply to Batch 2 if you meet eligibility — defer Batch 3 only if genuinely not ready.
