IIM Calcutta Executive Programs 2026 — MBAEx, PGPEX & Certificate Program Guide

August 31, 2026 · 6 min read · iimonlineprogram.in

IIM Calcutta — India's #2 Management School

IIM Calcutta (IIM-C) is NIRF #2 in Management, one of only two Indian business schools with Triple Crown accreditation (AACSB + AMBA + EQUIS), and the oldest IIM (established 1961). For working professionals, IIM Calcutta offers two full-time executive degree programmes (MBAEx and PGPEX) and various executive certificate programmes. The IIM-C brand carries the second-strongest management credential in India.

MBAEx vs PGPEX — What Is the Difference?

Both are 1-year full-time campus programmes at IIM Calcutta. MBAEx (MBA for Executives) gives an MBA degree — UGC recognised degree from IIM Calcutta. PGPEX gives a Post Graduate Diploma — equivalent in industry recognition but a diploma, not a degree. The curriculum and admission process are similar; the degree vs diploma distinction is the primary difference. Both require you to leave your job for 1 year at IIM Calcutta campus, Joka, Kolkata.

IIM Calcutta Certificate Programmes for Working Professionals

For professionals who cannot take 1 year off, IIM Calcutta offers shorter certificate programmes covering Analytics, Finance, Leadership, and Digital Business. These are online-compatible and give IIM Calcutta alumni status at ₹1-3L fee range. Availability and programme details change annually — verify directly with IIM Calcutta's executive education page.

IIM Calcutta vs IIM Nagpur Blended MBA — Key Distinction

IIM Calcutta MBAEx and PGPEX require 1 year of full-time campus attendance — meaning you must quit your job. IIM Nagpur Blended MBA (₹12.10L, 2 years) allows you to work full-time throughout the programme with only 4 × 5-day campus visits. Frequently Asked Questions

Does IIM Calcutta offer courses for working professionals?

Yes — IIM Calcutta offers MBAEx and PGPEX (1-year full-time, requires leaving job) and shorter certificate programmes online (3-6 months, compatible with work). The full-time executive programmes are for experienced professionals who can take a career break. The certificate programmes allow working alongside the study.

Is IIM Calcutta MBAEx worth it?

Yes — IIM Calcutta MBAEx gives a UGC-recognised MBA degree from India's #2 management school with Triple Crown accreditation. The 1-year full-time format means quitting your job and significant income loss — total cost ₹28-35L fees plus 1 year foregone salary. Best for professionals with 5+ years who can afford the career break and benefit from IIM-C's strong placement network.

Which IIM has MBA for working professionals without quitting job?

Only IIM Nagpur currently offers a full MBA degree programme for working professionals without requiring full-time campus attendance. The Blended MBA (₹12.10L, 2 years) has 4 × 5-day campus visits at IIM Nagpur — all other time is online evenings while continuing to work. All other IIM MBA programmes (MBAEx, PGPEX etc.) require full-time campus residency.
