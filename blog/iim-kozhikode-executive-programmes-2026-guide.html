IIM Kozhikode Executive Programmes 2026 — All 4 Programs Compared

August 10, 2026 · 7 min read · iimonlineprogram.in

IIM Kozhikode — India's #3 IIM for Working Professionals

IIM Kozhikode is ranked #3 in India by NIRF Management — EQUIS and AMBA accredited, making it one of only two IIMs with EQUIS accreditation (IIM Ahmedabad is the other). It offers 4 distinct executive programmes for working professionals, spanning senior leadership, analytics, strategy, and supply chain — the widest range of any single IIM at accessible fee points.

NAMP — For 10+ Years Experience

The New Age Management Programme for Senior Professionals is IIM Kozhikode's flagship senior leadership credential — the only IIM programme built exclusively for 10+ year professionals. ₹4L + GST, 10 months. Covers strategic leadership, M&A, digital transformation, and C-suite effectiveness. Peer cohort of 10+ year professionals is a significant quality differentiator.

Analytics & GenAI — Best Value IIM-K Programme

The most popular IIM-K programme in 2026 — ~₹2.1L for 6-8 months. Covers GenAI for business, analytics strategy, and data-driven decisions. No coding required. IIM-K's NIRF #3 / EQUIS+AMBA accreditation makes this one of the strongest analytics credentials available at this price point in India.

Strategic Management and Supply Chain — Specialist Options

Strategic Management (₹1.95L, 6 months) covers competitive strategy, digital transformation, and business model innovation — best for mid-level managers preparing for general management roles. Supply Chain Analytics (~₹1.75L, 6 months) is the most targeted IIM programme for operations/SCM professionals — covers demand forecasting, network optimisation, and digital supply chain.

IIM Kozhikode vs Other IIMs — Why Choose IIM-K?

  • NIRF #3 — higher ranked than most IIMs offering executive programmes
  • EQUIS accreditation — European accreditation stronger than AACSB for international use in some contexts
  • 4 distinct programmes — most choice per IIM among affordable options
  • NAMP is the only 10+-year-exclusive IIM programme at ₹4L (IIM-A AGMP is ₹8L for 5+ yrs)
  • Supply Chain programme — only IIM specialist supply chain analytics credential

Frequently Asked Questions

Which IIM Kozhikode programme is best?

It depends on experience level: 10+ years: NAMP (₹4L, 10 months). 2-7 years: Analytics & GenAI (~₹2.1L, 6-8 months) or Strategic Management (₹1.95L). Supply chain/operations background: Supply Chain Analytics (~₹1.75L). All give IIM-K alumni status.

Is IIM Kozhikode a good IIM?

Yes — IIM Kozhikode is ranked #3 in India by NIRF Management, EQUIS and AMBA accredited. It is firmly in the top 5 IIMs nationally. The EQUIS accreditation (held by only 2 IIMs — IIM-A and IIM-K) is considered the most rigorous international accreditation for business schools.

What is the difference between IIM Kozhikode NAMP and Analytics programme?

NAMP: 10+ years required, ₹4L, 10 months, strategic leadership for senior professionals. Analytics & GenAI: 2+ years required, ~₹2.1L, 6-8 months, AI/analytics for managers. Different experience levels, different curricula. Choose NAMP if 10+ years and targeting C-suite; choose Analytics if targeting AI-enabled management role.
