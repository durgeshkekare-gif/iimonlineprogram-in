IIM Nagpur vs IIM Ahmedabad — Which Program for Working Professionals 2026?

July 2026 · 6 min min read · iimonlineprogram.in

The most common comparison working professionals make: IIM Nagpur Blended MBA vs IIM Ahmedabad AGMP. Both are IIM programs. Both are accessible to working professionals. Both give IIM alumni status. So which one?

Short answer: If you want an MBA degree — IIM Nagpur. If you want India's most prestigious management certificate in the shortest time — IIM Ahmedabad AGMP.

The core difference — degree vs certificate

IIM Nagpur Blended MBA (2 years, ₹6.5L) gives you a UGC-DEB approved MBA degree — the same kind of degree as a full-time MBA from any IIM. IIM Ahmedabad AGMP (1 year, ₹3.85L) gives you an executive certificate — not a degree. Both give IIM alumni status.

For most career purposes in India, the distinction matters only when the role explicitly requires a 'degree'. For general career positioning and salary negotiation, both are highly effective.

When to choose IIM Nagpur Blended MBA

Choose IIM Nagpur when: you specifically want an MBA degree (not a certificate), you have 2-5 years of experience (AGMP requires 5+), you want the IIM alumni experience over 2 years including quarterly campus immersions, or you're in a field where the 'MBA degree' designation specifically matters.

When to choose IIM Ahmedabad AGMP

Choose IIM-A AGMP when: the IIMA brand is your primary objective — no other management credential in India has the same immediate recognition, you have 5+ years experience, you want to complete in 1 year, you can afford ₹3.85L and the EMI is manageable, or you're specifically targeting C-suite/board roles where IIMA is a known filter.

Both programs give you IIM alumni status and strong career positioning. The right choice comes down to: degree vs certificate, 2 years vs 1 year, newer IIM vs India's #1 IIM, ₹6.5L vs ₹3.85L. Our counsellors can help you map this to your specific situation.
