IIM Kozhikode Analytics & GenAI — What It Is

The Professional Certificate Programme in Analytics & Generative AI from IIM Kozhikode is a 6-8 month online certificate for working professionals. Fee: approximately Rs 2,10,000 + GST. No coding background required — it is designed for managers and business professionals who need to understand, use, and lead AI initiatives, not build them.

What You Learn

  • Data-driven decision making — reading dashboards, interpreting analytics, asking the right questions
  • Business analytics — regression, classification, clustering concepts without coding
  • Generative AI for business — ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude for productivity and decision support
  • AI strategy — evaluating AI vendors, building AI roadmaps, managing AI projects
  • Ethics and governance of AI — bias, privacy, regulatory considerations
  • Case-based learning — AI applications in BFSI, healthcare, retail, manufacturing

No Coding Required — What This Means

This programme is explicitly not a data science or machine learning course. It does not teach Python, R, SQL or model building. It teaches managers to work effectively with data teams, interpret AI outputs, evaluate AI vendors, and make strategic decisions in an AI-enabled environment. If you want to build models, see data science career guides. If you want to lead AI adoption — this is the right programme.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is IIM Kozhikode Analytics & GenAI certificate good?

Yes — IIM Kozhikode is India's #3 management school. The Analytics & GenAI certificate gives IIM-K alumni status and covers business analytics and AI strategy for managers. At Rs 2,10,000 for 6-8 months, it is the most prestigious non-technical AI certificate for business professionals in India.

Do I need coding for IIM Kozhikode Analytics programme?

No — the IIM Kozhikode Analytics & Generative AI programme requires no coding or programming background. It is designed for business managers and covers data-driven decision making, AI strategy and GenAI tools from a business and management perspective.

IIM Kozhikode Analytics or IIM Indore AI Cybersecurity — which is better?

Depends on your role. IIM-K Analytics & GenAI is better if your goal is AI strategy, business analytics and GenAI for productivity. IIM Indore AI & Cybersecurity is better if your organisation's focus is on AI risk, cyber governance and technology security. Both give IIM alumni status at similar fee levels.
