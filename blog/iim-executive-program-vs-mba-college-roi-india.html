IIM Executive Program vs MBA College — ROI Comparison India 2026

July 2026 · 8 min min read

A question that comes up constantly: should I do an IIM executive program or get an MBA from a good private college? The comparison sounds simple but has layers.

"The question is not IIM vs MBA college. The question is: what specific career outcome do you need, and which route gets you there?"

What you're actually comparing

IIM executive programs (6-11 months, ₹1.65L-₹3.85L) give you an IIM institutional credential and alumni status, designed for professionals already in their careers. Full-time MBA from a private college (1.5-2 years, ₹8-25L) gives you an MBA degree, campus placements, and a broad peer cohort.

When an IIM executive program wins

You're already 5+ years into a career in a field you want to stay in. You don't need placement — your career trajectory is your own to manage. The IIM brand alone is worth more to your career than the degree from a mid-tier private MBA. You cannot afford to lose 2 years of salary (~₹15-25L for most mid-career professionals). The fee differential (₹2-3L vs ₹15-20L) is significant.

Total cost of an IIM executive program: ₹2-4L in fees, zero income loss, done in 6-11 months. Total cost of a full-time private MBA: ₹12-25L in fees + ₹15-25L in foregone income = ₹25-50L total. You need that MBA to deliver ₹25-50L in additional lifetime earnings to break even — a high bar for many mid-tier schools.

When a full-time MBA wins

You're early career (0-5 years) and need placement infrastructure to change industry or function. You're targeting IIM-A/B/C specifically via CAT — the full-time programs there are worth the cost and sacrifice. You need an actual MBA degree for a specific role or international move. You can absorb the income loss.

For working professionals with 5+ years of experience who want to stay in their field: an IIM executive program almost always delivers better ROI than a full-time MBA from a mid-tier private college. The cost difference alone (₹2-3L vs ₹25-50L all-in) makes this the rational choice.
