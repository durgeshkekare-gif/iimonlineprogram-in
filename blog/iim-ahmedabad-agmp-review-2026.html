IIM Ahmedabad AGMP — What It Is

The Accelerated General Management Programme (AGMP) by IIM Ahmedabad is a 1-year executive certificate for working professionals with 5+ years of experience. ₹8,00,000 + GST (~₹9.44L all-in). Online with 3 campus modules at IIM Ahmedabad campus, Vastrapur, Ahmedabad. IIM Ahmedabad is ranked #1 in India by NIRF Management for 6 consecutive years, triple crown accredited (AACSB + AMBA + EQUIS). Gives IIM Ahmedabad alumni status — the most recognised management credential in India.

What the AGMP Curriculum Covers

  • Strategic management — competitive dynamics, corporate strategy, business model innovation
  • Financial management — financial statement analysis, investment decisions, valuation
  • Marketing — brand strategy, digital marketing, customer analytics
  • Operations — supply chain strategy, lean management, operations analytics
  • Leadership and OB — organisational behaviour, change management, leadership effectiveness
  • Digital transformation — technology strategy, AI for business, digital disruption
  • Capstone — strategy project applying all frameworks to a real business challenge

The 3 IIM Ahmedabad Campus Modules

AGMP includes 3 mandatory campus modules at IIM-A's Vastrapur campus — one of India's most iconic academic campuses. Each module is approximately 5 days. Campus immersions include intensive case discussions, faculty interaction, guest sessions from India's leading business leaders, and peer networking. The IIM-A campus experience is a significant part of the programme's value.

Honest Assessment — Who AGMP Is For and What It Delivers

What works: AGMP's single biggest value is IIM Ahmedabad alumni status — the most recognised management credential in India, period. The peer cohort of 5+ year professionals is genuinely diverse and high-quality. The campus experience at IIM-A is exceptional.

Be realistic about: AGMP is a certificate, not a degree. At ₹9.44L for a certificate, it is expensive relative to IIM Kozhikode NAMP (₹4.72L) or IIM Trichy SMP (₹4.25L). The value justification is entirely the IIMA brand — if you need India's #1 management institution credential, AGMP delivers it. If IIM brand (not specifically IIMA) is sufficient, lower-cost options give strong value.

"AGMP is the correct choice if you specifically need IIM Ahmedabad brand. For any other IIM brand: IIM Kozhikode NAMP at ₹4.72L delivers comparable management credibility at half the cost."

Frequently Asked Questions

Is IIM Ahmedabad AGMP worth the ₹8L fee?

AGMP at ₹8L (₹9.44L with GST) is justified for professionals who specifically need IIMA institutional brand for their career goals. IIM Ahmedabad is India's #1 ranked management institution — the alumni credential carries the highest recognition in corporate India. If IIMA brand specifically is required: yes. If 'any IIM' is sufficient: IIM Kozhikode NAMP (₹4L) delivers strong value at half the cost.

How many campus visits are required for IIM Ahmedabad AGMP?

AGMP requires 3 mandatory campus modules at IIM Ahmedabad campus, Vastrapur, Ahmedabad — each approximately 5 days. Plan 3 weeks of combined leave/WFH across 1 year. Accommodation on-campus or near IIM-A campus in Ahmedabad.

What is IIM Ahmedabad AGMP pass rate / completion rate?

IIM Ahmedabad does not publicly publish AGMP completion or attrition rates. Based on the programme's structure and fee commitment (₹9.44L), candidate drop-out rates are extremely low — professionals who invest this amount are highly motivated to complete. Academic performance standards are maintained; candidates who miss too many sessions or fail assessments may not receive the certificate.
