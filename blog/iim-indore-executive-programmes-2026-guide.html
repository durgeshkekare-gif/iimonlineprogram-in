IIM Indore — India's #4 Ranked Management Institution

IIM Indore is ranked #4 in India by NIRF Management and is AACSB accredited — placing it among India's top 5 IIMs. It currently offers one flagship executive programme for working professionals: the Executive Programme in Artificial Intelligence & Cybersecurity for Organizations. IIM Indore alumni status is a strong credential, particularly for tech-adjacent management roles.

Why IIM Indore for AI + Cybersecurity?

IIM Indore's unique positioning: the only IIM that combines AI strategy and cybersecurity governance in a single 6-month programme. This combination is increasingly demanded at board level — every organisation has both AI initiatives and cyber risks to govern. The AACSB accreditation and NIRF #4 ranking give IIM Indore alumni status significant market recognition.

What the Programme Covers

  • AI for business: GenAI applications, AI strategy, AI investment evaluation
  • Cybersecurity governance: threat landscape, risk frameworks (ISO 27001, NIST)
  • Board communication: presenting AI and cyber risk to non-technical boards
  • DPDP Act implications: India's data protection law for organisations
  • AI ethics and responsible AI: bias, fairness, governance frameworks
  • Vendor management: evaluating AI and cybersecurity vendors and contracts

Frequently Asked Questions

Is IIM Indore a top IIM?

Yes — IIM Indore is ranked #4 in India by NIRF Management and is AACSB accredited (same accreditation as IIM Ahmedabad). Established in 1996 by the Ministry of Education. It is firmly in India's top 5 IIMs in terms of brand recognition and academic quality.

What is the fee for IIM Indore executive programme 2026?

IIM Indore AI & Cybersecurity for Organizations: ~₹1,95,000 excluding GST. Total all-in with GST approximately ₹2.30L. Application fee separately. Verify current batch fee on Jaro Education official programme page.

IIM Indore vs IIM Kozhikode — which is better?

Both are top IIMs: IIM Indore (#4 NIRF, AACSB) and IIM Kozhikode (#3 NIRF, EQUIS+AMBA). IIM Indore is slightly lower ranked but has AACSB (American accreditation). IIM Kozhikode has EQUIS (European, generally considered more rigorous). For programmes: IIM Indore's AI+Cyber is a unique niche not available at IIM-K. IIM-K Analytics & GenAI is a broader business analytics programme.
