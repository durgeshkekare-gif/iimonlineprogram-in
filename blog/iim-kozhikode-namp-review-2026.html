IIM Kozhikode NAMP Review 2026 — Is It Right for 10+ Year Professionals?

July 2026 · 6 min min read
"Most management programs are designed for 25-year-olds. IIM Kozhikode NAMP starts where your career already is."

What is IIM Kozhikode NAMP?

The New Age Management Programme for Senior Professionals (NAMP) is IIM Kozhikode's executive program specifically designed for professionals with 10+ years of work experience. It runs for 11 months, is delivered online with campus immersion at IIM-K, and gives IIM Kozhikode alumni status on completion.

What makes it distinctive: the '10+ years experience' requirement is not a loose guideline — it is baked into the curriculum design. The program assumes you already understand operations, execution and function-specific expertise. It builds strategic leadership, business transformation, and C-suite readiness on top of that foundation.

Is IIM Kozhikode NAMP worth it — the honest view

What it delivers well

NAMP is one of the most honestly positioned IIM programs — it doesn't try to be a general MBA for everyone. Its 10+ year requirement means your cohort consists of established professionals at a similar career stage. The peer learning in that room is genuinely different from programs where you're sitting next to someone with 3 years of experience.

The curriculum is designed around strategic leadership challenges — P&L management, business transformation, leading in uncertainty — not foundational management basics that you already know after a decade.

What to be realistic about

NAMP is a certificate, not a degree. At ₹2.85L it is good value for an IIM credential, but go in with clear expectations: this gives you an IIM-K certificate and alumni status. It does not give you a degree, and it does not include placement support.

Bottom line: IIM Kozhikode NAMP is the most specifically calibrated IIM program for the 10-15 year professional. If that's you — and you want a quality IIM credential at ₹2.85L in 11 months — this is the clearest choice at that experience level.
